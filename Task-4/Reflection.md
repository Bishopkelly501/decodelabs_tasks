# Project 4 Reflection

## What I Learned

This project helped me understand the basic principles of PLC-based industrial automation.

I learned how digital inputs from push buttons and sensors can be mapped to a control system and how digital outputs can control motors, pneumatic pushers, and indicator lights.

## Conveyor Control

I implemented a sequence that starts the conveyor, detects boxes, classifies them according to size, and activates the appropriate pneumatic pusher.

The simulation successfully sorted two small boxes and one large box.

## Safety

The Emergency Stop was an important part of the project.

When the E-Stop was activated, the conveyor motor and pneumatic pushers were immediately switched OFF and the system entered a safe stopped condition.

The E-Stop test passed successfully.

## Challenges

One challenge was coordinating the sensor inputs with the conveyor and sorting outputs.

Another challenge was ensuring that the safety interlock had priority over normal operation.

## Result

The completed simulation demonstrated:

- Digital input mapping
- Digital output mapping
- Conveyor sequence control
- Box classification
- Pneumatic sorting
- Emergency Stop safety interlock

## Conclusion

This project improved my understanding of industrial automation and PLC-style control logic.

It showed me how sensors, actuators, sequence control, and safety systems work together to create a reliable automated production process.
